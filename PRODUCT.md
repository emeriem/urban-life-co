## Platform

web

## Users

- Community members and customers discovering Urban Life Co. experiences, events, and stories.
- Urban Life Co.'s Super Admin managing public content, events, and enquiries in the private CMS.

## Product Purpose

Urban Life Co. is an Enugu-rooted lifestyle ecosystem that creates experiences and communities to help people live, connect, and explore.

## Positioning

The specific differentiator beyond its experiences and community focus is undecided.

## Operating Context

The public-facing site supports discovery of experiences, events, journal stories, community, and contact. A private Super Admin CMS supports day-to-day content and event operations and enquiry management.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing React/Vite web app is a progressive web app.
- Preserve existing public and CMS workflows while improving their interfaces.
- The long-term multi-brand platform is a roadmap direction, not a capability to present as implemented. Tenant isolation is not established.
- Deployment is managed through AppDeploy; UI work does not imply approval to deploy.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Urban Life Co.
- Tagline: “Live. Connect. Explore.”
- Urban Hikers is an existing community and experience.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing public site and CMS implementation in `src/App.tsx`.
- Existing brand, event, experience, and journal copy in the app and project documentation.
- Do not invent testimonials, customers, performance claims, or proof points.

## Product Principles

- Help people discover real experiences and connect with the community.
- Preserve working public and administrative workflows.
- Distinguish implemented capabilities from roadmap plans.
- Treat operational safety and platform security as product constraints.
